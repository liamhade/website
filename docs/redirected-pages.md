# Context

There is a visual disconnect between the hero page and the other pages on my site.

# Goal

Update the design of the website so that the portfolio, poetry, music, and any other page that we may add in the future is stylistically consistent with the hero page.

# Design

When we click on one of the links on the hero page, the clouds and icons should be pushed to the right, and the land and links should be pushed to the left. The content of the page should then be displayed on an opaque background with rounded corners such that the star / sunny background can still be seen at the edges, without interfering with the content of the page.

It should feel like the page content is resting on top of clouded glass, that sits on top of the star / sunny backround.
